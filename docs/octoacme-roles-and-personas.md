# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance (QA) Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define quality standards, test strategies, and ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release. They partner with product and development teams to validate requirements and minimize defects.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and QA approach for each initiative
- Create acceptance criteria checklists and validation plans
- Execute or coordinate manual and automated testing
- Identify and triage defects, with severity and priority assessment
- Validate fixes and coordinate regression testing
- Provide quality metrics and test coverage reports

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features that meet user expectations
- Reduce production defects and support burden
- Establish repeatable, efficient quality processes
- Enable fast, confident releases

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement (defining acceptance criteria)
- Daily standups (test status and blockers)
- PR reviews (QA sign-off before merge)
- Release coordination (final validation and sign-off)

### How They Interact with Other Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Collaborates on test design, defines edge cases, validates fixes
- **Works with Product Managers**: Refines acceptance criteria, validates against requirements
- **Works with Project Managers**: Reports on quality metrics and test timelines
- **Works with Technical Leads**: Aligns test strategy with technical architecture

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads define technical direction, review design decisions, and manage technical debt. They provide technical guidance to the development team and ensure solutions align with organizational standards and scalability requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Review and approve technical design decisions
- Identify and prioritize technical debt
- Mentor developers on technical best practices
- Ensure code quality, performance, and security standards
- Participate in capacity planning and infrastructure discussions

### Goals
- Build scalable, maintainable systems
- Reduce technical risk and debt accumulation
- Enable team velocity through clear technical direction
- Foster a culture of continuous technical improvement

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and guidance
- Sprint planning (identifying technical complexity)
- Escalations on technical blockers or architectural decisions

### How They Interact with Other Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Guides on technical design, reviews complex work, mentors on standards
- **Works with Project Managers**: Advises on technical feasibility and timeline impacts
- **Works with QA Leads**: Validates testability of designs and technical approaches
- **Works with Operations/DevOps**: Collaborates on infrastructure and deployment strategies

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors represent business interests, provide strategic context, and ensure projects align with organizational goals. They approve scope changes and unblock resource or priority constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Articulate business objectives and success metrics
- Approve or recommend scope changes and trade-offs
- Unblock organizational or budget constraints
- Provide visibility to leadership and other executives
- Participate in key decision gates (initiation, planning, release)

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Align product roadmap with strategic priorities
- Mitigate organizational risks and dependencies
- Maximize return on investment

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval gate
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Ad-hoc escalations for blocking issues
- Release announcements and business impact updates

### How They Interact with Other Roles
- **Works with Product Managers**: Aligns priorities with business strategy and organizational goals
- **Works with Project Managers**: Reviews risks and timelines; approves major trade-offs and scope changes
- **Works with Delivery Team**: Provides context in kickoff; reviews demos and releases
- **Works with Executive Leadership**: Reports on project status and business impact

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads ensure solutions are user-centered, accessible, and usable. They conduct user research, design interfaces and workflows, and advocate for the user experience throughout the product development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and gather user feedback
- Design user interfaces, workflows, and information architecture
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Ensure accessibility and usability standards are met
- Collaborate with developers on implementation quality
- Measure and iterate on design effectiveness

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden
- Establish consistent design systems and patterns
- Drive user satisfaction and adoption

### Typical Communication
- User research findings and insights
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- Sprint planning (defining user experience requirements)
- Usability testing and iteration feedback

### How They Interact with Other Roles
- **Works with Product Managers**: Translates customer needs into user-centered designs and requirements
- **Works with Developers**: Collaborates on implementation details and ensures design fidelity
- **Works with QA Leads**: Defines acceptance criteria for usability and accessibility
- **Works with Project Managers**: Advises on design timelines and user research needs

---

## Operations/DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Operations and DevOps Engineers manage infrastructure, deployment pipelines, monitoring, and production support. They enable reliable, scalable systems and reduce the time from development to production deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain infrastructure and deployment environments
- Build and optimize CI/CD pipelines
- Monitor system health, performance, and security
- Manage production incidents and support escalations
- Document runbooks and operational procedures
- Ensure compliance and disaster recovery readiness

### Goals
- Ensure high availability and system reliability
- Reduce deployment risk and time-to-production
- Maintain cost-effective infrastructure
- Enable team self-service and rapid iteration

### Typical Communication
- Deployment coordination and release readiness
- Incident response and post-mortem reviews
- Infrastructure planning and capacity discussions
- Monitoring dashboards and alerts

### How They Interact with Other Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Supports deployment, troubleshoots production issues, advises on scalability
- **Works with Technical Leads**: Collaborates on architecture and infrastructure decisions
- **Works with Project Managers**: Provides input on deployment timelines and release windows
- **Works with QA Leads**: Coordinates staging environments and smoke test execution
